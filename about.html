About Nextrung

Nextrung is a small, independent brand from Australia. The name comes from the next rung on a ladder: you don't have to climb the whole thing today, just the next step.

It's run by a 23-year-old business student who works as a hospitality shift leader, trains at the gym, skates and loves cars. Every guide here started as something I had to figure out the hard way: walking into the gym for the first time, running a busy shift, getting assignments in on time, buying a car.

The guides are short, practical and printable. They're written in plain language, with checklists and worksheets so you can act on them straight away. AI writing tools help with drafting, and every guide is edited, checked and designed before it goes on sale.
