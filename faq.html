FAQ

How do I get my guide?

After checkout you get an instant download link by email and on screen. Nothing physical is shipped.

What format are the guides?

A4 PDF files. They work on phones, tablets and computers, and you can print them at home or at a print shop.

Can I print the worksheets more than once?

Yes, print as many copies as you need for your own use.

Can I share or resell a guide?

No. Each purchase is a personal licence. Please don't share, resell or upload the files.

Do you offer refunds?

See the refund policy. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law always apply.

Is this professional advice?

No. The guides are general information, not medical, legal, financial or professional advice. Each guide explains when to check with a professional.

Do you use AI?

Yes. AI writing tools help draft the guides. Every guide is then edited, checked and designed before it goes on sale, and we say so on each product.

Payment didn’t work or the download link is broken. What now?

Contact us and we'll sort it out.
