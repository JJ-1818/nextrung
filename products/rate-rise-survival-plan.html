Look inside

Rates in the news, and you're not sure what it means for your budget? The Rate-Rise Survival Plan turns "rates might go up" into one clear number: what a change would cost your household each month.

You'll map your last 30 days of money, run three quick stress tests (a small rise, a bigger rise and a quiet month), then pick from 15 practical ways to find the money. It walks you through asking your lender for a better rate with a simple script, using an offset or fortnightly repayments, and planning for a fixed rate ending.

It's made for Australia, with a renter's chapter (state rent rules and where to check them) and the free, official help available if things get tight, including Moneysmart, the National Debt Helpline and AFCA.

  • Mortgage holders on variable or ending fixed rates
  • Renters and households feeling cost-of-living pressure

Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. It's general information and worksheets to help you understand your own numbers. For advice about your situation, speak to a licensed adviser or a free financial counsellor (National Debt Helpline, 1800 007 007).

I rent. Is it useful for me?

Yes. There's a renter's chapter with state rent rules and where to check them, and the money snapshot, stress tests and savings finder work for any household.

Does it tell me what rates will do?

No one can promise that. It helps you plan for different scenarios, so you're ready whichever way rates move, and shows where to check the RBA's latest decisions.

Are the repayment figures today's rates?

They're clearly labelled examples using a 6.00% starting rate. The guide shows you how to get your exact figure with the free Moneysmart calculator in about 10 minutes.
