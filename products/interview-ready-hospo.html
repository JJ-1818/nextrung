Look inside

Applying for your first casual job can feel like everyone wants experience you don't have yet. In fact, most managers hiring for cafés, restaurants, fast food and shops care more about whether you're reliable, friendly and keen to learn. Interview Ready shows you how to prove that.

It takes you through the whole process in order: sorting out your TFN and availability, writing a one-page resume (even with no work history), walk-ins and online applications, common interview questions with simple answer frameworks, group and video interviews, trial shifts and what Fair Work says about unpaid trials, and copy-and-adapt follow-up messages.

It's written in plain Australian English for teens and young adults, with printable worksheets so you finish with your own resume, your own answers and a tracker of every place you've applied. No hype and no promises, just a clear plan.

I've never had a job. Is this for me?

Yes. It's written for people applying for their first job, and shows you how to use school, sport, volunteering and family responsibilities as real examples.

Does it write my resume or answers for me?

No. It gives you frameworks, examples to adapt and worksheets so your resume and answers are in your own words, which sounds better in an interview.

Is it only for Australia?

It's written for Australia and points to Australian official sources like the Fair Work Ombudsman and the ATO. The interview and resume tips work elsewhere, but the rules and pay information are Australian.

Will this guarantee me a job?

No guide can promise that. What it gives you is a clear plan and practice tools so you go into every application and interview prepared.
