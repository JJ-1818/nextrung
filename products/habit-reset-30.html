Look inside

Big resets usually ask for too much on day one, and a single late night can knock the whole thing over. The 30-Day Step-Up works the other way: one small action a day, each one easy enough to finish even when you're tired.

Week 1 steadies your sleep, starting with one wake-up time. Week 2 adds short, regular movement, like a 10-minute walk and a simple bodyweight set. Week 3 builds focus with 25-minute blocks and fewer notifications. Week 4 links what worked into a morning starter and an evening shutdown. Days 29 and 30 help you choose three habits to keep.

Every step has a plain-English reason and a smaller version for low-energy days. There are practical tips for shift workers, students and chaotic weeks. It's a guide with printable worksheets, not a daily planner, so there are no pages to fall behind on.
