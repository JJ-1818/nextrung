Look inside

Casual pay goes up and down, and Christmas doesn't wait for a good fortnight. December brings gifts, catch-ups and summer plans all at once, and January often brings thinner rosters and the same old rent. Christmas on Casual Pay helps you plan the whole season around pay that moves.

You'll find your floor (the lowest pay you can count on), set one Christmas number and a gift list that fits it, and learn how to check the public holiday lines on your payslip using the official Fair Work tools. Then you'll give your extra holiday shift money a job with a simple 50 / 30 / 20 split, and build a January bills checklist so the new year doesn't catch you out.

It follows one worked example from start to finish, written in plain Australian English with printable worksheets in every chapter. General information only, with links to official sources like fairwork.gov.au and moneysmart.gov.au.
