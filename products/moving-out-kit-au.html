Look inside

Moving out for the first time means applications, a lease, a bond, a condition report and bills you've never dealt with before. It's easy to miss a step that costs you money later. The First Place Kit walks you through all of it in order, in plain English.

It covers the whole move: working out the real upfront cost, viewing places and spotting scams, building a strong application with no rental history, reading the lease, how the bond works in each state and territory, doing a condition report that protects your bond, setting up power and internet, moving day, your first shop and your first month of bills.

Every chapter has a checklist or worksheet you fill in as you go, so by the end you have one folder with every record that matters. Written for young Australians renting for the first time. No jargon, no hype.
